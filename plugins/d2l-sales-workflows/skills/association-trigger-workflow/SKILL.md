# Association Trigger Workflow

Find credible outreach triggers for association and member-education accounts. The goal is to help
Pat decide what to work now, what to revisit, and what to suppress.

## Launch Modes

- `weekly-trigger-scan`: rank association accounts by current signal strength.
- `one-association-review`: research one organization and decide outreach readiness.
- `low-signal-summary`: identify accounts to suppress or revisit later.
- `follow-up-prep`: package evidence and suggested next touch.
- `research-priorities`: decide the narrowest checks for uncertain accounts.

## Association Signal Types

Look for:

- certification, credentialing, CE, or professional development programs
- member education modernization
- LMS, learning platform, or digital learning clues
- conference, event, or learning initiative announcements
- regulatory/compliance education pressure
- leadership changes affecting learning/member experience
- revenue or engagement pressure tied to education programs

## Decision Rules

Classify accounts as:

- `Work Now`: credible fit plus timely trigger and usable angle.
- `Light Research`: plausible fit but missing one important fact.
- `Revisit Later`: fit exists but timing is weak.
- `Suppress`: poor fit, weak evidence, duplicate, stale, or no D2L angle.

## Methodology

1. Define the association set, scan window, and launch mode before researching.
2. Collect only timely, attributable signals that could change outreach priority.
3. Separate association fit, trigger freshness, D2L angle, confidence, and missing proof.
4. Rank accounts by seller usefulness, not by the amount of research found.
5. Suppress stale, generic, duplicate, or poor-fit accounts with a one-line reason.

## Final Deliverable

Return ranked accounts with evidence, trigger type, confidence, outreach angle, next check, and
suppression reason where applicable. Include a short `weekly_sales_block` summary that names the
best accounts to work now, the narrow research checks to run next, and the accounts to ignore.
