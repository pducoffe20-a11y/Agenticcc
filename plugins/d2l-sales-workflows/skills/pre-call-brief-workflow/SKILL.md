# Pre-Call Brief Workflow

Build a sharp, seller-useful pre-call brief. Optimize for what Pat should know, ask, and do in the
meeting, not for exhaustive research.

## Launch Modes

- `calendar-brief`: brief from upcoming meeting context.
- `thread-brief`: brief from email/thread notes.
- `account-snapshot`: summarize account, business context, and likely priorities.
- `contact-angle`: infer stakeholder angle carefully from role and evidence.
- `discovery-plan`: produce discovery questions and call plan.
- `objection-prep`: prep likely objections and responses.
- `opener-next-step`: write the opening, objective, and proposed next step.

## Methodology

1. Confirm the meeting goal, participants, account, date, and source material available.
2. Read calendar, email/thread context, notes, CRM snippets, and public context only as needed for a
   useful call plan.
3. Separate confirmed facts, likely assumptions, and unknowns before writing the brief.
4. Translate evidence into what Pat should know, ask, say, and request next.
5. Keep the brief tactical enough to use minutes before the call.

## Final Deliverable

Return:

- `meeting_objective`
- `account_snapshot`
- `contact_context`
- `why_now`
- `likely_pains_or_priorities`
- `discovery_questions`
- `value_angles`
- `objection_prep`
- `opening_line`
- `next_step_to_ask_for`
- `unknowns_to_confirm`

## Evidence Rules

Separate confirmed facts from assumptions. If using email or calendar context, cite the meeting or
thread evidence briefly. Do not invent buyer priorities, budget, incumbent systems, or urgency.

## Tone

Keep it tactical, short, and usable five minutes before a call.
