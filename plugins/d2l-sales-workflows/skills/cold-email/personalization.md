# Personalization

Use personalization only when it connects to the reason for outreach.

## Levels

- Level 1: role or segment context.
- Level 2: company or organization signal.
- Level 3: timely event, initiative, post, hiring, program, or funding signal.
- Level 4: direct quote, customer overlap, or highly specific business trigger.

## Rules

- The opener should still make sense if the buyer reads it quickly on a phone.
- The observation must lead naturally into the problem or reason to reach out.
- Do not flatter, over-interpret, or mention trivia.
- If the signal is weak, use lighter language: `noticed`, `may be relevant`, `if this is on your radar`.

## Final Deliverable

Attach the personalization source or note the assumption. If a claim needs checking, put it in
`personalization_notes` or `review_flags` instead of copy-ready language.
