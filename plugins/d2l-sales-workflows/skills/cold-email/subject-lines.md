# Subject Lines

Subject lines should feel like they came from a colleague, not a campaign.

## Rules

- 2-4 words when possible.
- Lowercase is usually fine.
- No fake `Re:` or `Fwd:`.
- No urgency tricks, emojis, or first-name gimmicks.
- Prefer the buyer's topic over D2L's product.

## Patterns

- `CE programs`
- `member learning`
- `credentialing question`
- `[organization] training`
- `quick thought`
- `follow-up from [event]`

## Final Deliverable

Return 2-4 options when subject choice matters. Do not bury the recommended one; put it first.
